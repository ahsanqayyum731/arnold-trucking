About ARNOLD'S TRUCKING LOGISTICS LLC

A Yellow Hino Box Truck Fleet Built On Trust & Speed

ARNOLD'S TRUCKING LOGISTICS LLC was established by Rodney Arnold with a commitment to offering high-caliber box truck transportation and logistics across the United States. Headquartered in Huntersville, North Carolina, we operate with active regulatory authorities (USDOT #4314007 and MC #1682000).

Specializing in commercial yellow Hino 26ft Box Trucks equipped with hydraulic liftgates, e-track cargo tie-downs, and air-ride suspensions, ARNOLD'S TRUCKING LOGISTICS LLC delivers palletized cargo, retail merchandise, and expedited loads safely and punctually.
